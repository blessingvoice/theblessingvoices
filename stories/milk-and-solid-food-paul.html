Parables & Metaphors

Milk and Solid Food (Paul)

Reflect on spiritual growth through the comparison between milk and solid food.

1 Corinthians 3:1–3; Hebrews 5:12–14

Introduction

Read this English paraphrase of the biblical teaching alongside the original companion booklet. Context notes, devotional applications, reflection questions, and prayer are study aids rather than additional biblical text.

The Bible Story

Paul told the Corinthians that he had fed them milk rather than solid food because they were not ready, pointing to jealousy and quarrelling as signs of immaturity. This paraphrase follows 1 Corinthians 3:1–3. Hebrews 5:12–14 separately uses milk and solid food to discuss learning, maturity, and discernment developed through practice.

Historical and Biblical Context

CONTEXT & DEVOTIONAL INTERPRETATION

The milk image appears in two different letters with distinct emphases. Paul wrote 1 Corinthians. Hebrews does not name its author, so this page does not assign its wording to Paul. The illustration concerns spiritual maturity, not advice about diet.

Spiritual Lessons

CONTEXT & DEVOTIONAL INTERPRETATION

Learning should bear fruit in conduct. The passages connect maturity with moving beyond division and developing discernment through practice.

Practical Life Applications

CONTEXT & DEVOTIONAL INTERPRETATION

Choose a passage to study carefully and put one lesson into practice. Examine whether jealousy or quarrelling is hindering growth in your relationships.

Reflection Questions

  1. What behaviour does Paul identify as evidence of immaturity?
  2. How does Hebrews connect discernment with practice?
  3. What lesson could you put into action this week?

Closing Prayer

Lord God, help us understand your Word with humility. Teach us to grow in faith, care for one another, and put what we learn into practice. Amen.
