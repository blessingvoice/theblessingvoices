Jesus Heals Peter’s Mother-in-Law

Consider Jesus’ care for Peter’s mother-in-law and her response after the fever left her.

Matthew 8:14–15; Mark 1:29–31; Luke 4:38–39

Introduction

Read this English paraphrase of the Gospel account alongside the original companion booklet. Context notes, devotional applications, reflection questions, and prayer are study aids rather than additional biblical text.

The Bible Story

Jesus entered Peter’s house and saw his mother-in-law lying ill with a fever. He touched her hand, and the fever left her. She got up and began serving him. This paraphrase follows Matthew 8:14–15. Mark 1:29–31 and Luke 4:38–39 also record the healing.

Historical and Biblical Context

CONTEXT & DEVOTIONAL INTERPRETATION

This brief account places Jesus’ ministry inside a household. Matthew describes his touch; Mark describes him taking her hand and helping her up; Luke describes him rebuking the fever. Each Gospel’s account can be read without adding details about the household that are not given.

Spiritual Lessons

CONTEXT & DEVOTIONAL INTERPRETATION

Jesus’ care includes an individual’s need within an ordinary home. Her response invites devotional reflection on gratitude and service. The narrative does not make service a condition that must be met before receiving compassion.

Practical Life Applications

CONTEXT & DEVOTIONAL INTERPRETATION

Notice needs close to home. Offer practical support to a family member or neighbour. Express gratitude through a freely chosen act of kindness, while respecting the needs and limits of someone recovering from illness.

Reflection Questions

  1. What does Jesus notice when he enters the house?
  2. How does Peter’s mother-in-law respond after the fever leaves?
  3. What thoughtful act of care could you offer within your household or community?

Closing Prayer

Lord Jesus, thank you for caring about the needs of our homes. Teach us gratitude and help us support one another with gentleness. Amen.
