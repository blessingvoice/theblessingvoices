The Harvest

Explore harvest imagery in Jesus’ call to prayer and participation in God’s work.

Matthew 9:37–38; John 4:35–38

Introduction

Read this English paraphrase of the biblical teaching alongside the original companion booklet. Context notes, devotional applications, reflection questions, and prayer are study aids rather than additional biblical text.

The Bible Story

Jesus told his disciples that the harvest was plentiful but the workers were few, and urged them to ask the Lord of the harvest to send workers. This paraphrase follows Matthew 9:37–38. In a separate setting, John 4:35–38 records Jesus calling attention to fields ready for harvest and speaking of sowing, reaping, and shared labour.

Historical and Biblical Context

CONTEXT & DEVOTIONAL INTERPRETATION

Matthew’s saying follows Jesus’ compassion for the crowds. John’s harvest teaching appears after his conversation with the Samaritan woman. The passages share an image while belonging to different occasions.

Spiritual Lessons

CONTEXT & DEVOTIONAL INTERPRETATION

The image invites prayer, readiness to serve, and humility about work in which others have also laboured.

Practical Life Applications

CONTEXT & DEVOTIONAL INTERPRETATION

Pray for people who teach and serve. Offer a practical contribution to your community while acknowledging the work of others.

Reflection Questions

  1. What does Jesus ask the disciples to pray for?
  2. How does John’s passage describe shared labour?
  3. What practical contribution could you offer in service?

Closing Prayer

Lord God, help us understand your Word with humility. Teach us to grow in faith, care for one another, and put what we learn into practice. Amen.
