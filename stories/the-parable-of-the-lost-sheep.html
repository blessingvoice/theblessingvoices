The Parable of the Lost Sheep

Discover the joy of finding the lost sheep and Jesus’ teaching about care and repentance.

Luke 15:3–7; Matthew 18:12–14

Introduction

Read this English paraphrase of the biblical passage alongside the original companion booklet. Context notes, devotional applications, reflection questions, and prayer are study aids rather than additional biblical text.

The Bible Story

Jesus described a shepherd with a hundred sheep who lost one. He left the ninety-nine in the open country and searched until he found it. Joyfully he carried it home on his shoulders and called friends and neighbours to celebrate. Jesus connected this joy with joy in heaven over one sinner who repents. This paraphrase follows Luke 15:3–7.

Historical and Biblical Context

CONTEXT & DEVOTIONAL INTERPRETATION

Matthew 18:12–14 uses a related lost-sheep illustration in teaching about the little ones. Luke places it alongside the Lost Coin and the story of two sons. Each setting deserves attention.

Spiritual Lessons

CONTEXT & DEVOTIONAL INTERPRETATION

The illustration emphasizes seeking, the value of the one who is lost, and shared joy over repentance.

Practical Life Applications

CONTEXT & DEVOTIONAL INTERPRETATION

Notice someone who has become disconnected from community. Reach out with welcome and care rather than condemnation.

Reflection Questions

  1. What does the shepherd do after losing a sheep?
  2. How does Jesus explain the joy of finding it?
  3. How can you help someone feel welcomed rather than forgotten?

Closing Prayer

Lord God, help us hear your Word with humility. Guide our choices and teach us to respond with faith, mercy, and faithful action. Amen.
