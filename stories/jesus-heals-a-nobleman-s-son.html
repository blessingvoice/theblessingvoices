Jesus Heals a Nobleman’s Son

Explore the royal official’s trust in Jesus’ word before he learned his son had recovered.

John 4:46–54

Introduction

Read this English paraphrase of the Gospel account alongside the original companion booklet. Context notes, devotional applications, reflection questions, and prayer are study aids rather than additional biblical text.

The Bible Story

Jesus returned to Cana in Galilee. A royal official whose son was ill in Capernaum came and asked him to come and heal the boy, who was near death. Jesus told him to go because his son would live. The man believed Jesus’ word and departed. On his way, his servants met him with news that his son was living. He asked when the boy had improved and recognized it as the time Jesus had spoken. He and his household believed. This is a paraphrase of John 4:46–54.

Historical and Biblical Context

CONTEXT & DEVOTIONAL INTERPRETATION

The title nobleman is also rendered royal official. John identifies Cana and Capernaum and connects the recovery with the time of Jesus’ word. This account is distinct from the centurion’s servant story.

Spiritual Lessons

CONTEXT & DEVOTIONAL INTERPRETATION

The passage invites reflection on trust, concern for a child, and belief in Jesus. It records a particular healing rather than a formula for controlling outcomes.

Practical Life Applications

CONTEXT & DEVOTIONAL INTERPRETATION

Bring family concerns honestly to God. Support someone facing uncertainty with patient companionship and practical kindness.

Reflection Questions

  1. What does the official ask Jesus to do?
  2. How does he respond to Jesus’ word?
  3. How could you support a family facing uncertainty?

Closing Prayer

Lord Jesus, thank you for your mercy. Help us listen to your teaching and respond to others with gratitude, patience, and compassion. Amen.
