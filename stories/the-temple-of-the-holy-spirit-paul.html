The Temple of the Holy Spirit (Paul)

Consider Paul’s teaching about the body, belonging to God, and honouring him.

1 Corinthians 6:19–20

Introduction

Read this English paraphrase of the biblical teaching alongside the original companion booklet. Context notes, devotional applications, reflection questions, and prayer are study aids rather than additional biblical text.

The Bible Story

Paul asks whether the believers know that their body is a temple of the Holy Spirit within them, received from God. He reminds them that they are not their own and were bought at a price, and calls them to glorify God in their body. This is a paraphrase of 1 Corinthians 6:19–20.

Historical and Biblical Context

CONTEXT & DEVOTIONAL INTERPRETATION

The passage appears within teaching about sexual conduct and belonging to Christ. It is not a claim that a physical building or a particular modern ritual is being described.

Spiritual Lessons

CONTEXT & DEVOTIONAL INTERPRETATION

The image emphasizes the dignity and responsibility of embodied life in relationship with God. It invites respect rather than contempt for the body.

Practical Life Applications

CONTEXT & DEVOTIONAL INTERPRETATION

Consider how your choices express respect for yourself and others. Set a thoughtful boundary or change a habit that conflicts with faithful conduct.

Reflection Questions

  1. How does Paul describe the body’s relationship to the Spirit?
  2. What does belonging to God call believers to do?
  3. What practical choice could express greater respect for yourself and others?

Closing Prayer

Lord God, help us receive your Word with humility and put it into practice. Shape our words, choices, and relationships with truth, faithfulness, and love. Amen.
