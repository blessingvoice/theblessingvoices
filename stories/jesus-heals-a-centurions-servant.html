Miracles

Jesus Heals a Centurion's Servant

Reflect on a centurion’s humble trust in the authority of Jesus’ word.

Matthew 8:5–13; Luke 7:1–10

Introduction

Read this English paraphrase of the Gospel account alongside the original companion booklet. Context notes, devotional applications, reflection questions, and prayer are study aids rather than additional biblical text.

The Bible Story

In Capernaum, a centurion sought help from Jesus for his servant, who was suffering at home. He said he was not worthy for Jesus to enter his house, but trusted that Jesus could heal with a word. Drawing on his experience of giving and receiving orders, he recognized Jesus’ authority. Jesus marvelled at his faith and told him to go; the servant was healed at that hour. This paraphrase follows Matthew 8:5–13.

Historical and Biblical Context

CONTEXT & DEVOTIONAL INTERPRETATION

Luke 7:1–10 records the appeal through Jewish elders and then friends sent by the centurion. Matthew presents the exchange directly. Read each account on its own terms rather than inventing conversations to merge the details.

Spiritual Lessons

CONTEXT & DEVOTIONAL INTERPRETATION

The centurion’s request combines concern for another person, humility, and confidence in Jesus’ authority. The account draws attention to faith beyond the boundaries people might expect.

Practical Life Applications

CONTEXT & DEVOTIONAL INTERPRETATION

Pray for someone else’s needs and offer practical help. Reflect on the difference between trust and demanding a particular outcome. Approach others with humility, regardless of status.

Reflection Questions

  1. What does the centurion believe about Jesus’ authority?
  2. How does his concern for his servant shape his request?
  3. Who could you support through prayer and practical kindness this week?

Closing Prayer

Lord Jesus, deepen our trust in you. Teach us humility and help us care for others with patience and love. Amen.
