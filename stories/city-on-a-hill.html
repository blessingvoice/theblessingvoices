Parables & Metaphors

City on a Hill

Explore the image of a visible city within Jesus’ teaching about light.

Matthew 5:14–16

Introduction

Read this English paraphrase of the biblical teaching alongside the original companion booklet. Context notes, devotional applications, reflection questions, and prayer are study aids rather than additional biblical text.

The Bible Story

Within his teaching that his disciples are the light of the world, Jesus observed that a city situated on a hill cannot be hidden. He went on to describe a lamp giving light and called for good works that lead others to glorify the Father. This is a paraphrase of Matthew 5:14–16.

Historical and Biblical Context

CONTEXT & DEVOTIONAL INTERPRETATION

The city image is one part of the same passage studied in Light of the World and Lamp on a Stand. The text does not name a particular city or identify the modern location shown in the thumbnail.

Spiritual Lessons

CONTEXT & DEVOTIONAL INTERPRETATION

The illustration invites attention to the visible influence of a community’s shared life. The surrounding verses connect that influence with good works and praise to God.

Practical Life Applications

CONTEXT & DEVOTIONAL INTERPRETATION

Consider one way your family, church, or study group can offer visible, practical care. Let consistency between words and actions guide the effort.

Reflection Questions

  1. Why is a city on a hill a useful image of visibility?
  2. What does the surrounding passage say about the purpose of good works?
  3. How could your community serve others more faithfully?

Closing Prayer

Lord God, help us understand your Word and receive it with humility. Guide our choices and teach us to live with faith, wisdom, and love. Amen.
