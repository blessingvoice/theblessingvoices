Jesus Heals a Man with Leprosy

Discover Jesus’ compassionate response to a man who asked to be made clean.

Matthew 8:1–4; Mark 1:40–45; Luke 5:12–16

Introduction

Read this English paraphrase of the Gospel account alongside the original companion booklet. Context notes, devotional applications, reflection questions, and prayer are study aids rather than additional biblical text.

The Bible Story

A man with leprosy came to Jesus and pleaded that, if Jesus was willing, he could make him clean. Jesus reached out, touched him, and expressed his willingness. The man was immediately cleansed. Jesus told him to show himself to the priest and offer what Moses commanded. This paraphrase follows Matthew 8:1–4; parallel accounts appear in Mark 1:40–45 and Luke 5:12–16.

Historical and Biblical Context

CONTEXT & DEVOTIONAL INTERPRETATION

The account includes both the man’s plea and Jesus’ instruction to go to the priest. The Gospel writers describe cleansing as well as healing. The biblical term commonly translated as leprosy should not be used here to diagnose a modern medical condition.

Spiritual Lessons

CONTEXT & DEVOTIONAL INTERPRETATION

The account invites reflection on Jesus’ compassion, the man’s trust, and obedience to Jesus’ instructions. The man approaches with need rather than a claim to entitlement.

Practical Life Applications

CONTEXT & DEVOTIONAL INTERPRETATION

Bring your needs honestly to God in prayer. Notice people who feel excluded and offer respectful, practical care. Let compassion shape the way you speak about people who are suffering.

Reflection Questions

  1. What do you notice about the man’s request?
  2. How does Jesus respond to him?
  3. How could you show respectful compassion to someone who feels excluded?

Closing Prayer

Lord Jesus, thank you for your compassion. Help us bring our needs to you and treat people who suffer with kindness and dignity. Amen.
